Cheating & Penalties

Incident Levels

Minor 1–2 point deduction. Slight rule breaking that affects a result, cheating, too many complaints from other players.
Major 3–5 point deduction. Outlandish cheating or rule breaking that clearly affects a result.
DQ Disqualification from event — decided by group vote for extreme cases — followed by a Major incident penalty scaled up to 5 points.

Team vs Individual Cheating

  • If a whole team cheats — every member on the team loses points.
  • If an individual cheats on a team — the individual loses 100% of the reduction; all other team members lose 50% of the reduction.

Bye Policy

Each player is allowed up to 2 byes throughout the season. Using a bye means skipping an event without it negatively affecting your standings.


If a majority of players end the season having used both of their byes (i.e., most people missed 2 events), then everyone removes their lowest-scored event result from their total points. This keeps standings fair when absences are common.

Using a bye: Let the commissioner know in advance if you're skipping an event so it can be logged correctly.
